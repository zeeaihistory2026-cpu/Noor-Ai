# NOOR AI Companion — Long-Term Organic Growth Roadmap

**Project decision (2026-10-08):** NOOR is a long-term project, not a one-time app launch. The owner plans to grow it mainly through organic short-form content on TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook because short Islamic reminder videos already travel well on those platforms.

**Important truth first:** people may love Islamic reels, but reels alone do not create a lasting app. The winning strategy is a loop:

> **Useful app → beautiful shareable card/reel → viewer sees NOOR branding → installs NOOR → uses it daily → earns a streak/progress → shares again.**

This plan focuses on that loop. It does not depend on paid advertising. Paid promotion can be tested later, but it is not the foundation.

Religious-content rule for the whole plan: keep text in marketing material to brief excerpts with the correct reference. Full Qur'an and Hadith text belongs in approved app/API sources, not in this roadmap or in production scripts copied without checking.

---

## 1. The growth engine

### 1.1 The daily loop

1. A user opens NOOR for prayer, Qur'an, Dhikr, Hifz, or a daily reminder.
2. NOOR creates a beautiful, correctly referenced card or short reel from that moment.
3. The user shares it to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, or a story.
4. Every export carries a tasteful NOOR identity so a viewer knows where it came from.
5. The viewer installs NOOR, starts a streak, and later shares their own card or reel.

The Share/Reel Studio is therefore not a side feature. It is the bridge between private worship and public discovery.

### 1.2 Branding on every export

Every image or video export must include:

- the **NOOR** name;
- the tagline **Faith • Guidance • Growth** where space allows;
- the correct source reference for the Ayah, Hadith, or Dua;
- a clear but tasteful “Get NOOR” footer once store links exist;
- on reels, a small permanent NOOR mark in a safe corner plus a final 1-second NOOR end frame.

Branding should be elegant, never so large that it cheapens the religious message. The first job is to move the heart; the second job is to make NOOR easy to find.

Before public store links exist, exports can say **NOOR AI Companion** without a fake download link. Do not publish a link until it opens the real listing or an approved waiting-list page.

### 1.3 Daily content pillars

Use six repeatable pillars. This stops the team from asking, “What should we post today?”

| Pillar | What it is | Typical format | Main goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Ayah of the Day | A short, comforting or motivating Qur'anic reminder with reference | 7–15 second reel; image card for Feed/Facebook | Saves and shares |
| 2. Hadith of the Day | A brief authentic reminder with collection and number | 7–15 second reel; story card | Trust and repeat viewing |
| 3. Dua Moment | A simple supplication for morning, evening, anxiety, gratitude, family, forgiveness, or hardship | Text-on-screen reel; story with poll/question sticker where available | Emotional connection |
| 4. Streak Flex | A user's Namaz, Zikr, Qur'an, or Hifz progress card | Story image; “Day X” reel screenshot | App installs and return visits |
| 5. “What does the Qur'an say about…?” | A respectful demo of NOOR's referenced search: anxiety, patience, anger, gratitude, forgiveness, worry, hope, and similar everyday issues | 15–30 second screen demo + result card | Show the product doing something valuable |
| 6. Hifz Helper | Small memorisation and revision habits, weak-page planning, and encouragement | 15–30 second tip reel; before/after planner view | Serve committed learners and build authority |

**Weekly balance:**

- 3 × Ayah of the Day
- 2 × Hadith of the Day
- 2 × Dua Moment
- 2 × “What does the Qur'an say about…?” demos
- 2 × Hifz Helper posts
- 2 × streak/progress or community posts
- 1 × rest, repost of a proven performer, or a live/community question

That is usually 1–2 posts per day. Do not post seven weak items to look busy. A sustainable rhythm beats a short burst followed by silence.

### 1.4 What every post needs

Each piece should have:

1. **A hook in the first 1–2 seconds.** Ask a recognisable human question or show the result first. Example structure: “Feeling anxious today?” → brief reminder and reference → NOOR result/share card.
2. **Captions/subtitles.** Many people watch silently. Burn clear subtitles into every reel.
3. **A correct reference on screen.** Trust is the brand.
4. **One action only.** Choose one: save this, share with someone who needs it, start your streak in NOOR, or comment with tomorrow's topic. Do not ask for five actions.
5. **A pinned first comment** with the full reference and, after launch, the store link.
6. **A matching app moment.** If the reel shows anxiety support, the app card/search result should be ready for the viewer after installation.

## 2. Phased roadmap

## Phase A — Prove it on a phone and with the first real users

**Timeframe:** now until the app passes the phone checklist and a small beta.

### A1. Phone proof

Complete the existing phone-run checklist before spending serious effort on launch content:

- splash opens into Home;
- real local prayer time and next prayer are correct;
- Qur'an Arabic, English, and Urdu display and resize correctly;
- audio is heard clearly; play, pause, skip, and speed work;
- Salah taps and Tasbih counting work;
- Qibla behaves sensibly with real movement;
- the prayer reminder rings with the app closed and after reboot;
- creating and sharing a card opens the phone share sheet;
- Save Image stores a real PNG;
- streak cards use the user's own streak values.

Any failure becomes a fix task before beta. Do not hide device problems behind more features.

### A2. Small beta: 20–50 users

Recruit a manageable group: family, friends, students, mosque/community contacts, teachers, and a few people who already post Islamic content.

Ask each tester to complete five missions over seven days:

1. check prayer times and set one reminder;
2. read or listen to a short Qur'an passage;
3. complete one Dhikr session;
4. use the referenced search once;
5. create and share one card or streak image.

**Record in plain words:**

- Did it install without help?
- Did anything look wrong or confusing?
- Which feature did they open again without being asked?
- Did they share anything? If yes, why? If no, what stopped them?
- What one thing would make them keep NOOR after Ramadan or after the test week?

**Phase A exit gate:** 20–50 real users tested, the reminder/audio/Qibla either pass or have named fixes, at least 10 users return on a second day without a reminder from the owner, and no content-accuracy mistakes are found in the sampled cards/search results.

### A3. Prepare the launch kit during beta

- app icon and store screenshots from the real phone;
- short store description and longer description;
- privacy page and support contact;
- TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook profile names/handles reserved consistently where possible;
- profile photo, bio, and link page;
- a bank of the first 30 approved reels/cards so launch week does not depend on daily production panic;
- a simple spreadsheet: date, pillar, hook, reference, format, caption, hashtags, result.

Do not announce a public launch date until the phone gate and beta gate pass.

## Phase B — Launch with a steady content rhythm

**Timeframe:** the first 30–45 days after the store listing is live.

### B1. Store listing

The listing should sell the daily benefit, not list 42 screens. Suggested title direction (already recorded for the PM board):

> **NOOR – Quran, Prayer Times & Duas**

The first screenshot should show the beautiful Home/prayer value. The next screens should show Qur'an, referenced AI finding, Hifz, and share cards. Every screenshot caption should answer “What do I get?” in one short line.

Use the actual app, actual references, and actual results. Do not claim a full AI brain, scholar approval, or a feature that is not connected.

### B2. Daily publishing rhythm

Target **1–2 strong posts per day**:

- one main reel;
- one story/feed card or a second short reel when production is easy.

Repurpose intelligently:

- TikTok: original post;
- Instagram: Reel + Story card;
- Facebook: Reel + Feed image/post;
- WhatsApp Status/Channel if used later: the same card, because it is highly shareable in the owner's community.

Do not simply dump identical captions everywhere. Keep the visual, then adapt the first line and call-to-action to the platform.

### B3. Thirty-day content calendar outline

Use this four-week rotation and fill each row with an approved, referenced card/search result from NOOR.

| Day pattern | Pillar | Hook idea | On-screen result | Call to action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | What does the Qur'an say? | “What does the Qur'an say about anxiety?” | Referenced result card | “Save this for a hard day.” |
| Tue | Ayah of the Day | “A reminder for the one who feels alone…” | Ayah card + reference | “Share with someone who needs it.” |
| Wed | Hifz Helper | “Your weakest page is not your enemy. Revise it first.” | Revision/heatmap view | “Start a gentle revision plan.” |
| Thu | Hadith of the Day | “One small deed, done consistently…” | Hadith card + reference | “Save and act on it today.” |
| Fri | Dua Moment | “A Dua before Jumu'ah…” | Dua card | “Send this to your family group.” |
| Sat | Streak Flex | “Day 12 of not missing Salah. Keep going.” | Namaz streak card | “Start your Day 1 in NOOR.” |
| Sun | Community question | “What should we search for tomorrow?” | Poll/question + best referenced answer next day | “Comment one topic.” |

Repeat with new topics and winning formats for four weeks. By day 30, compare results and keep only the best two or three formats as the core engine.

#### Hook structures that fit NOOR

- “What does the Qur'an say about **[issue]**?”
- “Read this before you give up on **[goal/habit]**.”
- “A 10-second reminder for **[emotion/situation]**.”
- “Day **[number]** of **[Namaz/Zikr/Qur'an]** — Alhamdulillah.”
- “Most people revise randomly. NOOR starts with the weak page.”
- “Save this Dua for **[morning/night/anxiety/gratitude]**.”
- “One Hadith. One action. Today.”

Hooks must remain truthful. Never promise healing, wealth, guaranteed acceptance of Dua, or a religious ruling in a hook.

#### Caption structure

Use the same four-part caption every time:

1. **Feeling/question:** name the viewer's moment.
2. **Reminder:** one short sentence about the referenced content.
3. **Reference:** exact Surah:Ayah or collection and number.
4. **Action:** save/share/start/comment — choose one.

Example template:

> For the heart that feels heavy today. A short reminder, with its source, so you can read more in NOOR.
>
> Reference: Qur'an [Surah:Ayah]
>
> Save this for the day you need it again.

#### Hashtag sets

Do not paste 30 random hashtags. Use 5–10 relevant tags and rotate sets.

**English core set:**

`#Quran #IslamicReminder #Muslim #Dua #Hadith #Salah #Dhikr #Hifz #IslamicReels`

**Topic sets:**

- Anxiety/comfort: `#IslamicReminder #QuranVerses #MuslimMentalHealth #TrustAllah #Sabr`
- Prayer: `#Salah #Namaz #PrayerReminder #MuslimLifestyle #Fajr`
- Hifz: `#Hifz #QuranMemorization #HifzJourney #QuranStudy #MuslimStudents`
- Family/sharing: `#IslamicQuotes #DuaForFamily #MuslimCommunity #DailyReminder`

**Urdu/Roman-Urdu set:**

`#اسلام #قرآن #دعا #نماز #ذکر #Hadis #IslamicReminderUrdu #Deen #Sabr #Alhamdulillah`

Check spelling and platform behaviour before each campaign; hashtags support discovery, but the hook, retention, saves, and shares matter more.

### B4. Community replies

For the first 60 days, reply like a careful human, not a brand bot:

- reply to meaningful comments within 24 hours;
- pin the correct reference if someone asks “source?”;
- thank people who share a personal benefit;
- invite one topic suggestion per day and answer the best one in the next reel;
- correct a mistake publicly and promptly if a reference or caption is wrong;
- never argue sectarian points in comments;
- move personal religious ruling questions to: “This needs a qualified scholar who knows your situation; NOOR can show sources, but it does not issue rulings.”

A weekly 20–30 minute question-and-answer Story or Live can begin after the audience asks for it. Do not start a Live series before there are repeat questions.

### B5. Launch success measures

At day 30, do not judge only by views. A small account with high saves and shares is healthier than one viral video with no installs.

Review:

- Which pillar brings followers?
- Which pillar brings app installs?
- Which reel gets saves (private value) versus comments (conversation)?
- Which share card is actually being sent by beta users?
- Does the app retain the people who arrive?

## Phase C — Turn repeat viewers into a community and creators

**Timeframe:** after launch, roughly months 2–6, adjusted by real results.

### C1. Series formats

Create named series so viewers recognise and return:

- **60 Seconds With the Qur'an** — one issue, one referenced result.
- **Hadith in 15 Seconds** — brief reminder + exact reference.
- **Weak Page Wednesday** — Hifz revision tip.
- **Day-One to Day-100** — owner or volunteers show streak progress honestly.
- **Dua Before…** — before sleep, before an exam, before work, before travel, before Jumu'ah.
- **Ask NOOR** — the audience chooses the topic; NOOR shows the referenced search result.

A series must have a consistent opening, subtitle style, sound policy, end frame, and posting day. Repeat what proves valuable; retire formats after a fair test (usually 6–10 posts) if saves/installs stay weak.

### C2. Collaborations with small Islamic pages

Start with small, trusted pages rather than chasing celebrities. Good partners:

- Qur'an/Hifz teachers;
- local mosque youth pages;
- Islamic reminder creators;
- Muslim student societies;
- family/parenting pages where Dua and habits fit;
- Urdu-language reminder pages for the home audience.

Offer a clear value exchange:

- NOOR creates a co-branded card/reel for their topic;
- the partner posts or duets/stitches where the platform allows;
- both pin the source reference;
- the partner receives the export without a large watermark covering the religious text;
- NOOR receives a clear credit and link in caption/bio during the campaign.

Before collaboration, review the partner's past content for accuracy, tone, and sectarian arguments. Never buy fake followers or pay for engagement pods.

### C3. User-generated streak-card challenges

Run simple, voluntary challenges:

- **7-Day Fajr Challenge**
- **100 Dhikr a Day for 7 Days**
- **One Page a Day Qur'an Challenge**
- **Weak Page Revision Week**

Rules:

- participation is private by default; sharing is optional;
- users post the NOOR streak/progress card, not personal worship details they may regret sharing;
- use one campaign hashtag and a story template;
- celebrate consistency and return after a missed day, not public ranking or shame;
- feature user cards only with explicit permission;
- do not turn worship into a competition leaderboard. NOOR's tone is encouragement, never exposure.

The owner can repost the best community cards (with permission) every Friday. This creates content without forcing the team to invent everything.

### C4. Build owned channels

Social platforms rent attention. Slowly build channels NOOR controls:

- in-app daily reminder;
- optional email or WhatsApp broadcast only with clear opt-in;
- a simple website/landing page with store links and the daily card;
- a searchable library of the best referenced topics.

Do not collect phone numbers or emails before privacy wording and consent are ready.

## Phase D — Monetisation at launch: free + limited ads, PKR 500/month Remove Ads (AI later)

**Owner decision (2026-10-08):** NOOR launches **free for everyone**, funded by **limited ads**, with a **PKR 500/month Remove Ads** subscription for users who want no ads. Suggested annual option: **PKR 3,999/year**. The **AI layer is a later phase** — not launch scope and not part of the launch price. At launch, the AI experience stays the honest referenced databank search/sample already built; Premium AI features, Hifz Pro, family/institution plans and supporter models may be revisited only after retention, costs and the AI layer are real.

**What “limited” means (the standing rule):**

- Cap: **≤3 light interstitials/day**, only at natural breaks, **never fullscreen on app open**.
- **Zero ads inside worship/reading experiences:** no ads during prayer/Salah flows, Qur'an reading, audio/recitation playback, Mushaf/Reader, or Hifz-test screens.
- **Banner-only** on Home/Library-type browsing surfaces.
- **Ramadan pledge:** during Ramadan days, **no interstitials at all — banner-only** on allowed surfaces, as a public trust pledge.
- Family-safe category controls (no gambling, alcohol, dating, sexual or misleading health/finance categories); reviewed before launch and monthly.
- Remove Ads upsell lives **only in Settings/Profile** (one tap into the store subscription sheet). No nag popups, modals, countdowns or blocked content.
- The free tier keeps the **full core**: prayer, Qur'an/Reader, reminders, Qibla, Salah/Tasbih tracking and referenced search are never paywalled.

**Rewarded ads (voluntary, Share Studio/Settings only, max 3/day):** a user may choose to watch a completed ad to unlock a premium share-card/reel design for 24 hours, extra card designs, or an **“ad-free until tomorrow.”** Rewarded ads never gate Qur'an/prayer/reminders/core content and never interrupt recitation or reading. They are an increment for users who cannot (or prefer not to) pay; PKR 500/month stays the effortless, no-watching anchor. Watch the cannibalisation honestly: if “ad-free until tomorrow” becomes a daily habit for would-be subscribers, Remove Ads conversion will soften.

**Later (not launch):** once the AI layer exists and retention proves people stay, paid value can be reconsidered in this order: Premium AI/Hifz features, family plans, pay-it-forward/supporter, then institutions — each with its own accuracy, privacy and cost review.

Ethical rules (unchanged, now encoded as ad policy):

- do not sell user worship data;
- do not use guilt, fear, or “streak loss” pressure to sell;
- be transparent about what Remove Ads changes (ads off) and what stays free (the whole core);
- correct any ad-policy breach publicly and fix the placement before re-enabling it.

## Phase E — Long-term product and content maturity

**Timeframe:** after the first growth loop works, roughly month 6 onward.

### E1. AI backend at scale

Move from labelled sample/free-form stand-ins to a server-side AI service only when the project can support it safely:

- user accounts and secure tokens;
- source retrieval before explanation;
- scholar-reviewed answer patterns for common issues;
- rate limits and cost controls;
- abuse and medical/crisis safeguards;
- answer logs reviewed for accuracy (without exposing private user data unnecessarily).

NOOR may explain sources; it must not claim to be a Mufti or replace a scholar.

### E2. More languages and audiences

Prioritise based on real installs/comments, not guesses. Likely order:

1. Urdu experience and Roman-Urdu captions;
2. Arabic interface/content review;
3. English improvements for international users;
4. additional languages only when translation and religious review capacity exists.

Do not machine-translate sacred explanations at scale without review.

### E3. Scholar-reviewed content pipeline

Build a small repeatable queue:

1. topic suggested by app data/comments;
2. source found in approved Qur'an/Hadith databank;
3. brief excerpt and explanation drafted;
4. reference checked by a second person;
5. qualified scholar/teacher reviews sensitive topics;
6. approved card/reel published;
7. corrections logged and fixed visibly if needed.

Keep a record of reviewer, date, source, and version for every reusable content pack. This protects trust as volume grows.

## 3. What the app must grow next to serve the strategy

Ordered backlog. Build only the next item after the phone/beta gate shows it is needed.

### Priority 1 — Real MP4 reel export

**Why:** TikTok/Reels/Facebook growth needs a real video file, not only an animated preview.

Include:

- 9:16 export at a platform-ready size;
- soft text reveal/fade and optional gentle zoom;
- subtitles;
- 1-second NOOR end frame;
- export progress and honest failure state;
- no copyrighted recitation or background track unless rights are cleared.

### Priority 2 — Daily auto-generated reel/card templates

**Why:** one-tap daily content removes production friction.

Include:

- today's Ayah, Hadith, and Dua automatically placed into approved templates;
- streak/progress template after a completed session;
- one-tap “Create today's reel” and “Create today's card”;
- reference locked on the design so users cannot accidentally remove it;
- owner review before posting, never silent auto-posting.

### Priority 3 — Caption and hashtag generator in Export

**Why:** the share moment is where users decide whether to post.

Include:

- platform choices: TikTok, Instagram, Facebook;
- caption using the approved four-part structure;
- English, Urdu, and Roman-Urdu variants;
- topic hashtag sets (5–10 tags);
- Copy Caption button beside Share/Save;
- reminder to verify the reference before posting.

### Priority 4 — Gentle in-app share reminders after real moments

**Why:** remind users when value is fresh, not with random pop-ups.

Trigger only after:

- a streak milestone (Day 7, Day 30, personal best);
- completing a Dhikr target;
- finishing a Hifz revision session;
- reading/listening to a meaningful passage and tapping a “share this” action.

The prompt should be dismissible, never block worship, and never shame a missed day.

### Priority 5 — “Get NOOR” deep-link footer on cards

**Why:** a beautiful card without a path back is lost marketing.

Include:

- small footer with NOOR name and store link or QR code where appropriate;
- one link that later sends Android/iPhone users to the correct store;
- campaign code in the link so we know which series brought installs;
- footer hidden from the sacred text area and kept visually quiet.

### Priority 6 — Content-performance feedback loop

- record which pillar/template generated a share (inside the app only, with privacy respect);
- a simple owner view: most shared cards, most searched issues, most saved reminders;
- weekly export of counts for the PM review;
- no invasive tracking and no sale of religious behaviour data.

### Priority 7 — Creator and community kit

- co-branded template for approved partners;
- challenge templates (7-Day Fajr, One Page a Day, Weak Page Week);
- “feature my card” permission flow;
- downloadable media kit for small Islamic pages.

## 4. Honest risks and non-negotiable rules

### 4.1 Religious accuracy is the brand

One wrong reference can destroy trust built by 100 good reels.

Rules:

- every Ayah/Hadith/Dua card carries an exact reference;
- brief excerpts only in scripts/cards; full text comes from approved sources;
- two-person check for every new content pack;
- qualified scholar/teacher review for interpretation, sensitive issues, Madhhab matters, health, finance, marriage, and personal rulings;
- no invented chains, numbers, translations, or “the Prophet said” claims;
- corrections are posted openly, the wrong asset is replaced, and the correction is saved in the content log;
- comments requesting a personal fatwa are directed to a qualified scholar.

### 4.2 Platform rules change

TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook can reduce reach, remove sound, flag re-uploads, or change link behaviour without warning.

Rules:

- keep original project files and captions;
- post natively to each platform;
- do not rely on one account or one format;
- maintain the app, landing page, and opt-in list as owned channels;
- follow each platform's current music, branded-content, health-claim, and religious-content rules at posting time;
- never use misleading hooks, engagement bait, fake comments, or purchased followers.

### 4.3 Audio and visual rights

Do not use a reciter's recording, nasheed, lecture clip, image, or calligraphy in marketing unless the licence clearly allows commercial app promotion.

Safer order:

1. NOOR-owned/generated design assets made for the project;
2. properly licensed audio/images with written terms saved;
3. public-domain or clearly Creative-Commons material whose licence permits this use;
4. partner-supplied material with written permission.

Keep licence files beside the assets. “It was on the internet” is not permission.

### 4.4 Sustainable cadence over viral gambling

A viral reel can help, but it is not a strategy. The plan is:

- one useful reminder at a time;
- repeat proven pillars;
- improve hooks and subtitles from data;
- answer comments;
- make sharing effortless;
- retain users with a genuinely useful daily app.

Protect the owner's time. Batch-produce once or twice a week, schedule safely, and keep one rest/repost slot. If quality or accuracy drops, reduce quantity.

### 4.5 Privacy and dignity

- streaks and worship habits are sensitive; sharing is always user choice;
- never expose a user's missed prayers publicly;
- get permission before reposting a user's card, name, face, or voice;
- children need parent/guardian permission and extra care;
- crisis or self-harm topics must not be answered with a generic religious script alone; use platform safety resources and encourage real-world help according to the user's location.

## 5. KPIs in plain words + weekly review ritual

## 5.1 The few numbers that matter

| KPI | Plain meaning | Where to see it | Early healthy direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Installs | People who put NOOR on a phone | Store reports | Rising week by week after launch; exact target set after beta conversion is known |
| Cost per install | Money spent to get one user | Ads/store reports if ads ever run | Organic should be near-zero cash cost; track owner time honestly |
| Day-1 retention | Installed yesterday, opened again today | App analytics | Improving after onboarding fixes; investigate every sudden drop |
| Day-7 retention | Still using after one week | App analytics | A key truth metric: the daily habit is forming |
| Day-30 retention | Still using after one month | App analytics | The long-term project signal; premium should not be considered before this is understood |
| Activation | New user completes prayer setup + one useful action in first session | App analytics | Most beta users should finish without owner help |
| Shares per active user | How many cards/reels an active user sends out | In-app share counts + platform reports | Rising after Priority 1–4 app features ship |
| Reel reach | Unique people who saw a reel | TikTok/Instagram/Facebook | Useful, but secondary to saves/shares/installs |
| Saves per 1,000 views | People kept the reminder for later | Platform analytics | Strong signal of emotional/useful value |
| Shares per 1,000 views | People sent it to someone else | Platform analytics | Strongest organic growth signal |
| Follower-to-install rate | Followers who become app users | Link/store campaign data | Shows whether branding/footer is working |
| Content accuracy incidents | Wrong reference, translation, or claim found | Content log | Target: zero; any incident triggers review before more posting |

Do not set vanity targets before beta. First learn the real baseline. After 30 days, set next month's target from actual numbers (for example, improve Day-7 retention or shares per user by a modest percentage) rather than copying another app's numbers.

### 5.2 Weekly PM review — 30 minutes, same day each week

Bring one page only:

1. **Numbers:** installs, Day-1/Day-7/Day-30 retention, activation, shares per active user.
2. **Best three posts:** pillar, hook, saves/1,000 views, shares/1,000 views, installs if known.
3. **Worst three posts:** was it hook, topic, design, timing, or production quality?
4. **User words:** three real comments/questions and one beta/user complaint.
5. **Accuracy log:** anything corrected or awaiting scholar review.
6. **One decision:** keep, change, or stop one thing next week.

Rule: change one major variable at a time. If hooks, design, posting time, and pillar all change, nobody knows what worked.

### 5.3 Monthly decision

At month-end, the PM answers five questions:

1. Which pillar creates retained users, not just views?
2. Which feature gets opened again without prompting?
3. Are users sharing without us begging?
4. What did accuracy or support cost us in time?
5. What is the single bottleneck for the next month: phone trust, content, app feature, retention, or backend scale?

Fund that bottleneck next. Everything else waits.

## 6. Immediate next steps

1. Finish the phone proof.
2. Run the 20–50-user beta.
3. Prepare the launch kit and first 30 approved posts during beta.
4. Launch the store listing with 1–2 strong posts per day.
5. After real sharing behaviour appears, build app Priority 1 (real MP4 reel export), then Priority 2 (daily auto-generated templates).
6. Review weekly; let retained users and honest shares, not views alone, decide what grows next.

**Long-term promise:** NOOR grows by being useful every day, accurate every time, and easy to share after a meaningful moment. That is slower than chasing a viral trick — and much more durable.
