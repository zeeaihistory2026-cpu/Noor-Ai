# Azan alert

`android/app/src/main/res/raw/azan.ogg` is the unmodified 42-second recording
"Adhan.ogg" by Aishatu98 (15 July 2026), released under CC0 1.0.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adhan.ogg

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This bundled recording is used for Android prayer-time notification sounds.
Android notification/channel settings, silent mode, and battery restrictions
can affect playback. It is an azan alert, not a live mosque broadcast.

Daily Quran listening uses the existing Alafasy recorded ayah source.
Dua and Hadith listening uses the device's Arabic text-to-speech voice and is
labelled as device speech in the interface.
