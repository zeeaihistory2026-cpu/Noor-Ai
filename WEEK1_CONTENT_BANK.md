# NOOR — Week 1 content bank (first 7 posts, ready to make)

Prepared 2026-10-08 from the app's own Phase 14 card library
(lib/data/share_content.dart) and the weekly rotation in
GROWTH_ROADMAP.md §B3. Every text below is a brief excerpt already
in the app, with its reference printed — nothing new was written
from memory, and no reference was invented.

**When to use this:** GROWTH_ROADMAP Phase A3 puts the first-30
content bank in the beta window, after the phone run. This bank is
ready-made so launch week never depends on daily production. The
Friday Dua (Day 5) can be pulled forward to any Friday — including
tomorrow, Fri 9 Oct — if the social accounts are opened early.
Before store links exist, cards say "NOOR AI Companion" only; no
download link until the real listing is live (roadmap §1.2).

**How each post is made in the app today:** Share tab → pick the
card → choose format → Export PNG → post via the phone's share
sheet. Formats already in the app: Reel/Story 1080×1920 (TikTok +
Instagram), Instagram Post 1080×1350, Facebook Post 1200×628.

## Rules kept from the roadmap (do not break)

- One action per post (save / share / comment / start) — never five.
- Reference always on the card and repeated in the pinned comment.
- No promises of healing, wealth, guaranteed acceptance of Dua, or
  religious rulings in hooks (§B3). Ruling questions get the
  scholar line from §B4.
- Brief excerpts only; full text lives in the app sources.
