# Install NOOR on your phone (2 minutes, taps only)

You will receive one file: **NOOR.apk**. No cable, no computer
skills — just follow these taps on your Android phone.

## Install

1. Open the file I send you (tap the download). If the phone asks
   where to save it, choose **Download**.
2. Open your **Files** app (or tap the download notification) and
   tap **NOOR.apk**.
3. If the phone says installs from this app are blocked, tap
   **Settings** on that popup → turn ON **"Allow from this
   source"** → go back. (This is Android's standard step for any
   app installed outside the Play Store; NOOR is your own test
   build.)
4. Tap **Install**. When it finishes, tap **Open**.
5. First open: Splash says **NOOR**, then the app leads you in.
   When the app asks for permissions, tap **Allow** for:
   - **Notifications** (so prayer reminders can ring)
   - **Location** (only when you open Qibla, so the arrow points
     correctly from where you stand)

That's it — NOOR is on your phone. 🎉

## Then do the test tour

Follow **PHONE_RUN_CHECKLIST.md** (the "test tour" list): open
each tab, listen to the audio, tap through Salah/Tasbih, try the
Qibla, and do the big reminder test (turn Fajr reminder ON, swipe
the app away, and see if it rings while closed).

For anything that fails or looks different from your design
pages, note the checklist step number (or take a screenshot with
Power + Volume Down) and tell me the number — that exact screen
gets fixed next.

## Good to know

- This is a **test build** (not yet the Play Store version). It
  may show a small "debug" flavour on some phones; everything
  inside works the same.
- Uninstall any time: long-press the NOOR icon → Uninstall.
- Your data (streaks, bookmarks, settings) lives on the phone.

---
