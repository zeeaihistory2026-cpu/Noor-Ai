# NOOR — Beta tester pack (ready to send, use AFTER the phone run passes)

Purpose: the 20–50 person beta in GROWTH_ROADMAP.md §A2 / PM_BOARD
(beta gate: 20 Dec 2026). Nothing here is sent yet — it is ready so
recruiting can start the day the phone proof passes.

## 1. Who to invite (20–50 people)

Family and friends first, then students, mosque/community contacts,
teachers, and 2–3 people who already post Islamic content. Android
phones first (the test build is Android).

## 2. Copy-paste invite (WhatsApp — English)

Assalamu alaikum! I'm testing NOOR AI Companion, a new Islamic app
(prayer times, Qur'an with Urdu, reminders, beautiful Ayah/Hadith
cards). Would you try it for 7 days? I'll send you one install
file — just tap to install, no cost, nothing to buy. Each day
there's one small 2-minute task. Your honest feedback will shape
the app before Ramadan. Jazakumullah khairan!

## 3. Copy-paste invite (Roman Urdu)

Assalamu alaikum! Main NOOR AI Companion test kar raha hoon — ek
nayi Islamic app (namaz ke auqaat, Urdu ke saath Qur'an, reminders,
khoobsurat Ayah/Hadith cards). Kya aap 7 din try karenge? Main ek
install file bhejunga — bas tap karke install karna hai, koi paisa
nahi. Roz ek chhota sa 2-minute ka task hoga. Aap ki sachhi raaye
Ramadan se pehle app ko behtar banayegi. Jazakumullah khairan!

## 4. The 5 missions (send one per day, Days 1–5)

1. Check today's prayer times and turn ON one reminder.
2. Read or listen to a short Qur'an passage.
3. Do one Tasbih/Dhikr session.
4. Ask the app: "What does the Qur'an say about patience?" (or pick your own topic).
5. Make one Ayah/Hadith card and share it with anyone.

Days 6–7: no tasks — we watch who opens NOOR again on their own.

## 5. Feedback questions (Day 7, plain words)

1. Did it install without help?
2. Did anything look wrong, confusing, or unlike what you expected?
3. Which part did you open again without being asked?
4. Did you share a card? If yes, why? If no, what stopped you?
5. What one thing would make you keep NOOR after the test week?

## 6. Simple tracker (one row per tester)

Tester | Phone (Android?) | Installed alone? (Y/N) | Missions done (of 5) | Opened again Day 6–7? (Y/N) | Shared a card? (Y/N) | Best quote / complaint

## 7. Exit gate (from GROWTH_ROADMAP §A2 — do not launch past this)

- 20–50 real users finished the week
- At least 10 opened NOOR again unprompted on a later day
- Reminder, audio and Qibla passed on real phones (or fixes are named and scheduled)
- Zero wrong references found in shared cards / search answers
